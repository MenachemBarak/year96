## The human experience (definition of done)

- **Done means:** the whole system runs by itself. It works by itself, heals itself and evolves itself. The human only gives input, freely, whenever they want.
- **Talk from any app:** the human writes from any communication app they already use (Slack, Telegram, WhatsApp, email, voice, web chat). The app is only a front end; it connects to whatever backend runs Year96.
- **See every thread:** one place shows all active threads in the system. The human can open any of them, join or just listen, and create their own thread to talk with a Communicator.
- **Watch the whole system live:** a dashboard shows the entire architecture, meaning its layers, agents, threads and state, and lets the human zoom from the big picture down to a single agent or event, live.
